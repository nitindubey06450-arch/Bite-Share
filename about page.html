🌱 The BiteShare Story

Every single day, local restaurants, bakeries, caterers, and residential households prepare wholesome, delicious meals—some of which inevitably go unsold or unused. At the same time, shelters, community kitchens, and low-income families struggle with food security.

BiteShare eliminates this disconnect. By utilizing real-time geolocation pings, strict food safety protocols, and verified NGO logistics, we make food rescue as simple as a single click. Whether you are a baker with extra artisan bread or a family with extra portions from a gathering, BiteShare ensures your surplus transforms into someone else's comfort and dignity.
